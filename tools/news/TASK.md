# NEWS task — "News from the running world" for @earnedmileshq (Metricool blogId 7489774)

Work fully autonomously; nobody is watching. Report to the user (Andrew, Czech speaker) in 2-3 short Czech sentences at the end.

GOAL: Publish a news carousel ONLY when there is a genuinely MAJOR or clearly on-brand running story, and make it as FRESH as possible. Most runs should publish NOTHING; that is the expected and correct outcome. SPEED MATTERS: Andrew wants @earnedmileshq to be the FIRST page to post a drop, a registration opening or a big result. This task runs HOURLY (08:13-22:13 Prague time). At most THREE news posts per day, at least 90 minutes apart; a daily live update of an ongoing event counts as one post. If a story qualifies, turn it into a 5-slide Instagram carousel (1080x1350) in the EARNED style and schedule it in Metricool within minutes (see step 5). (The old "News refresh" task is disabled; REFRESH.md is kept only for manual use.)

## 1) FIND NEWS
Use WebSearch (several queries, mode "extended" if results are thin) and open the most recent source pages with WebFetch to read the CURRENT numbers/state (always note the timestamp of each source; prefer pages updated in the last hours; for live events find the newest standings/leaderboard). A story qualifies only if it is one of these:
- Results or live status of a top championship or world-level event (e.g. Backyard Ultra World Championships (Team/Individual), World Athletics Championships, Olympic/Diamond League finals, World Marathon Majors, Western States, UTMB, Comrades etc.), especially winners, records, notable DNFs/finishes.
- A new world, continental or major-race course record in any distance (track, road, trail, ultra, backyard/last-man-standing laps record).
- Entry/lottery announcements for major half marathons and marathons (World Marathon Majors: London, Berlin, Chicago, NYC, Boston, Tokyo, Sydney; big European races such as Prague, Valencia, Vienna): lottery or ballot opening/closing/results, sell-out dates, big registration changes. Include dates and links to the official source in the sources slide.
- A notable new running shoe (flagship racing/super shoe or a clear breakthrough model from ANY brand): official launch, release date, price, key spec.
- DROPS from Andrew's favourite brands: 247, Adidas, Saucony, Satisfy, Tracksmith, Bandit, Soar, Nike, On (and other clearly comparable running/performance brands): new shoe/apparel collections, limited editions, collabs, restocks of hyped items, release dates and prices. Must be a real, officially announced drop, not just a colorway on an old model.
- EPIC ATTEMPTS and achievements: someone attempting or completing something extraordinary (e.g. running across a whole country such as Iceland, FKTs, record-length runs, multi-day or multi-country journeys, huge ultra feats). Cover announcements of an attempt, progress while it is live, and the result. Name the person in the carousel and tag them in the caption.
- Other genuine big-hype stories (major doping decisions, famous athlete retirement/comeback, major rule or technology changes).
Routine items do NOT qualify: minor colorway drops without a collab or hype, small local races, ordinary training news, listicles, opinions, rumors without credible sources.
FAST PATH: an announcement made by the owner of the news about itself (a brand announcing its own drop or shoe, a race opening/closing its own registration or lottery, a federation or organizer publishing results/records) is enough on the OFFICIAL source alone (official site, official press release, or verified official social account page that WebFetch can read); do not wait for a second source. For everything else (results reported by media, records, hype, doping, attempts, rumors) require at least 2 credible sources (official site counts as one).
Requirements (all stories): recent (last ~24-48h, or an announced date in the next days). Never invent facts or numbers; every figure must match the sources. Unconfirmed items must be phrased "reportedly". Put the as-of time/day into the carousel (e.g. "Day 5", "as of 21 Oct, 12:00 CEST") so the content is never misleading.
MULTI-DAY / LIVE EVENTS: if an ongoing event (e.g. the Backyard Ultra World Team Championship) already had a NEWS post on a previous day, publish a fresh DAILY UPDATE with the newest standings (title like "Backyard Worlds: day 6 update"), one per day, until the event ends, then publish one final RESULT post (winner, final numbers, records). Skip a day's update only if nothing materially changed.
Before committing, call Metricool getScheduledPosts (brandId 7489774, fromDate = 3 days ago, toDate = 3 days ahead, timezone Europe/Prague, ISO with offset like 2026-10-21T00:00:00+02:00). Skip if a post starting with "NEWS:" already covers the same story with the same state, or if 3 NEWS posts are already scheduled/published for today, or if a NEWS post is scheduled/published within 90 minutes of the time you would use (take the next free time slot instead, unless the story is time-critical).
If nothing qualifies: do not render or schedule anything. Report in one Czech sentence that nothing major was found.

## SOURCES (priority)
Prefer: official event/federation/brand pages (World Athletics, AIMS, World Marathon Majors sites, Big's Backyard Ultra / event sites, brand press pages), live timing/result platforms, and established running media (Runner's World, Canadian Running, LetsRun, CITIUS Mag, iRunFar, Marathon Handbook, Outside, Running Magazine, national press agencies / BBC / ABC / Reuters / AP for big events). Treat social media posts and forums only as a lead, never as the single source. Ignore content farms, undated pages, and unsourced rumor accounts. Always list the 2 best sources in the sources slide with the as-of date.

## 2) RENDERER
Work in tools/news of the cloned repo: run `npm install` there. Render with: PW=/opt/npm-tools/node_modules/playwright CHROME=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium-1194/chrome-linux/chrome node news.js spec.json out . See README.md and sample.json for the spec format (title, hook, icon, slides[3] with label/h/c/stat?/icon, sources[]). Use *asterisks* around the 1-3 key words to highlight in volt. Keep texts SHORT: title <= 8 words, hook <= 14 words, each slide caption <= 2-3 short sentences, stat = one number + 3-word label (for lotteries/entries use dates or numbers such as the opening date or number of places; for drops use release date or price; for attempts use distance/days). Slide labels: "What happened", "The numbers", "Why it matters" (entries/lotteries: "What happened", "Key dates", "How to enter"; drops: "What dropped", "Key details", "Why it's hyped"; attempts: "The attempt", "The numbers", "Why it matters"). Icons: shoe/shoe2/trailshoe for shoes, trophy/podium/finish/laurel for races and records, stopwatch for times, calendar for dates/lotteries, flame for hype/drops, globe/hill/runner for epic journeys, megaphone/newspaper otherwise. IMPORTANT: do NOT draw, recreate or embed any brand logo or product design, and do not download brand/press images. Brand, event, athlete, team and model names appear as plain text only. Language: English.

## CATEGORY COLOURS (mandatory)
Every news post has exactly ONE category; set these fields in the render spec (accent, accentDeep, hue, kicker). Volt green is reserved for educational carousels/stories, never for News. Pick by the main topic; for multi-day events use LIVE while running, then RECORDS with the final result.
- RECORDS & RESULTS (records, results, championships): "accent":"#FFB000","accentDeep":"#B37A00","hue":-31,"kicker":"NEWS · RECORDS & RESULTS"
- DROPS & SHOES (new shoes, collections, collabs, brand drops): "accent":"#FF3DCB","accentDeep":"#A82889","hue":241,"kicker":"NEWS · DROPS & SHOES"
- REGISTRATION (lotteries, registration opening/deadlines): "accent":"#4D8DFF","accentDeep":"#3162B3","hue":146,"kicker":"NEWS · REGISTRATION"

The highlight *asterisks* in texts use the accent colour automatically. Add "category" to spec.json too.
